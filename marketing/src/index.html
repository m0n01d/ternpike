How It Works

Three steps.
Then go to sleep.

01
📸
Snap your receipts
Photograph one or a stack at the end of a long driving day. Queue them all at once — Ternpike processes in parallel.
02
🤖
AI reads them
Claude extracts the amount, merchant, date, and category. Writes a short note and a detailed one. You review and confirm — or edit anything.
03
📍
Logged with GPS
Every entry gets coordinates — from the photo EXIF, your browser, or a map pin you drop. Your spending trail plots on a map of the whole route.
Features

Built for
the realities of remote travel.

📶
Works offline
Installable PWA. Queue receipts in the backcountry, sync when you hit signal. Cached data always viewable.
🗺️
Waypoint map
Every expense with coordinates plots on a live map. Your financial trail and your geographic trail in one view.
📊
Live stats dashboard
10 live metrics. Daily spending chart. Category breakdown. Cumulative spend curve. Know your burn rate at a glance.
🔒
Your data, your sheet
One Google Sheet is the entire database. You own it. Export to CSV, run formulas, connect to Looker Studio. We're a UI layer, not a data silo.
⚡
No account required
Google sign-in only — no passwords, no new accounts. Works without an API key too: photos queue for manual entry.
🏕️
11 road-trip categories
Fuel · Food · Camp · Ferry · Gear · Lodging · Activities · Shopping · Medical · Transport · Misc. Broad enough for months, specific enough to be useful.
