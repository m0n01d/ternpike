Ternpike — Mock Flock Seeder

Bumps your account to Fly tier and seeds a "Honeymoon" flock with an Italy trip and four expenses split between two members. Idempotent — safe to run again to reset.

You must already be signed in to the app at least once (so auth_creds exists in IndexedDB). If you've never signed in, run the app first.
